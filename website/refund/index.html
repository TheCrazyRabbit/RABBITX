Policy / Updated 20 October 2026

Refund Policy

New paid subscriptions are currently unavailable. The early trial is free and requires no payment. The information below is for any existing transactions.

Refund requests for RABBITX Pro subscriptions are handled through Paddle, the merchant of record for checkout purchases.

Request a refund

Use Paddle buyer support with the order information from your receipt. Do not post card details, receipts or personal billing information in public GitHub Issues.

Eligibility and processing

Refund eligibility and processing are assessed under Paddle’s Refund Policy, the applicable purchase terms and any mandatory consumer rights. This policy does not add a guaranteed refund period or an unconditional refund promise. Paddle handles approved refunds; contact buyer support for your order’s status.

Cancellation and renewal

You may request cancellation through Paddle buyer support or use the subscription-management link supplied with your billing communications. Cancel before the next renewal to prevent future billing. Cancellation does not by itself refund a previous payment; submit a separate refund request if needed.

Product questions

For general product questions, contact Crazy Rabbit using the link below. Billing requests requiring private transaction information should go to Paddle’s secure buyer support channel. Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot legally be excluded.

Talk to Crazy Rabbit

For product questions, beta feedback and policy questions, contact the maintainer through GitHub Issues. A GitHub account is required. Do not post credentials, payment details, private target URLs or security-sensitive evidence in a public issue.
